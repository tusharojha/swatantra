# Photo credits

Demo stills bundled under `app/web/seed/`. Resized and JPEG-compressed for the app
(under 150 KB each). The live file is never written to Arkiv — only a hash chain
root, district and 0.1° cell.

These photographs illustrate **issue types**. They are not pictures of the named
streets, and they are not pictures of the named desks. No private person is in
focus. Bylines in the app stay anonymous desk ids.
